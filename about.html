About me

The emotion of a comic book's panel layout is as manipulably impactful as any magazine, billboard, or even as the dynamic scenes of the comic panels themselves. Intentional, thoughtful, bewilderingly dramatic: comics are my origin story, as they lit my passion for graphic design! Beyond comics, I find inspiration everywhere — from the thunderous noise of unspoken connection surrounding us, to the bubbles clinging to their last throes of existence at the edge of a freshly poured glass of water.
